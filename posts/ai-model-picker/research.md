# Research — Which AI model for which job (October 2026)

Shazim's research brief, preserved verbatim for re-verification. Every number
on the slides comes from here. Do not change a price on a slide without
re-checking the source below.

Built on 10+ model launches in September 2026:
- OpenAI: GPT-6 Astra (3 Sep), GPT-6 Sol and Luna (22 Sep), GPT-6.1 Sol at DevDay (29 Sep).
- Anthropic: Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 (1 Sep), Opus 5.5 (22 Sep), Sonnet 5.5 (28 Sep).
- Google: Gemini 3.8 Flash (2 Sep), Gemini 4 Argon (30 Sep, trusted testers, reported $2/$10).

Prices and rankings, checked 5 Oct 2026 (USD per million tokens, input/output):
- Claude Opus 5.5: $4 / $20 (down from Opus 5's $5/$25). #1 on BenchLM's composite
  index; #1 for agentic work on llm-stats. Terminal-Bench 4.0: 66.4% vs 57.9% for
  GPT-6 Astra. 1M token context.
- Claude Opus 5.5 for motion/design: writes the animation as code and renders the
  video; reviewers rate its visual design the best tested; Charlie Hills calls
  motion "the biggest jump".
- Claude Sonnet 5.5 (28 Sep): $2 / $10. CursorBench 4.0 55.5% vs Opus 5.5's 57.8%;
  Terminal-Bench 4.0 70.6%. ~30% faster and up to 30% cheaper per task than Sonnet 5.
- Claude Fable 5.1: $10 / $50, long-running projects. Mythos 5.1: same model,
  trusted US organisations only.
- GPT-6 Astra: $10 / $50. GPT-6.1 Sol: $2 / $10 (a fifth of Astra), cached input $0.10.
- Gemini 3.8 Flash: $0.75 / $3.75 until 31 Dec 2026, then $1.50 / $7.50.
  Gemini 4 Argon: trusted testers only, $2 / $10 introductory.
- Worked example (our estimate): 3,000 emails/month at ~1,000 input tokens each
  = 3M input tokens. Astra ~$30, Sol ~$6, Gemini 3.8 Flash ~$2.
