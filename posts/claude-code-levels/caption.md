## Caption (paste this)

most people use claude code at level 1 and think they've seen it.

the actual ladder:

lvl 1 — terminal. plain english in, working code out.
lvl 2 — memory. CLAUDE.md so it stops asking about your stack.
lvl 3 — commands. /clear, /compact, /context — one keystroke actions.
lvl 4 — skills. your workflows, saved, called by name.
lvl 5 — mcp. plug it into notion, gmail, your database, your browser.
lvl 6 — subagents. a second claude doing the long job on the side.
lvl 7 — hooks. scripts that fire on events, automatically.
lvl 8 — headless. claude -p in CI — no chat window at all.
lvl 9 — routines. scheduled runs in the cloud while you sleep.

i run my whole content system on levels 4 and 9. most founders only ever need 1–5.

comment **LEVELS** and i'll DM you the roadmap — one small build per level, with the exact commands 👇
