## Caption (paste this)

22 slack-triggered workflows for content studio 9/16. hundreds of hours of manual work recovered.

openai and spacexai both put ai agents inside slack this autumn. here's what running automation there looks like in practice.

before: the studio read every ai newsletter by hand, scrolled creators for ideas, and wrote every script from a blank page.

what we built: a content os. 22 workflows, every one triggered from slack — because the team already lived there, and nobody had to learn a new tool.

the three that carry the studio:
1. news in, stories out — newsletters split into stories, each with a summary and why it matters
2. ideas on autopilot — a scraper checks creators and keywords twice a day and files every new idea
3. one click, one script — any idea becomes a 30, 60 or 90-second script or a linkedin draft, then moves into the weekly plan

built with n8n, slack and openai.

the rule we build by: fixed workflows for repeat work, agents for work that changes.
