## Caption (paste this)

google is replacing gemini's gems with skills. here's a six-step template for writing your first one.

claude has skills. gemini has skills now too. in chatgpt it's project instructions. same idea: saved instructions for one job.

1. pick one task you explain over and over — a refund reply, a proposal, a product description
2. describe what good looks like in two or three sentences
3. list your rules — tone, length, what to always include
4. paste three real examples you were happy with
5. list what it must never do — promise dates, quote prices, use words you'd never use
6. save it as a skill in claude or gemini, or as project instructions in chatgpt, then test it on a real case

an hour of writing, and every ai tool you use starts sounding like your business.

which task would you write down first? 👇
