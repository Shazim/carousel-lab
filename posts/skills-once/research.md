## Verified (web search, Oct 2026) — the 4 Oct date doesn't match
- Skills began rolling out in Workspace on 5 Oct 2026 and in the Gemini app on
  13 Oct 2026.
- Gems are retired from 17 Nov 2026 for personal accounts (auto-migrated that
  day), March 2027 for Workspace business/enterprise/nonprofit, June 2027 for
  Workspace education.
- Skills are invoked with "/" in the prompt box, can be stacked, and Gemini can
  invoke them automatically.
